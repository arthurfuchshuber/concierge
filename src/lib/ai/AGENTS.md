# AI rules
- Human escalations deduplicate only identical requests within a short retry window, scoped to owner/property/conversation; unrelated pending, proactive and learning cards must never suppress a new guest question.
- AI decision cards (`ai_human_escalations.context_snapshot.decision`) may carry whitelisted actions (`DECISION_ACTIONS`); the proactive copilot (`proactive-decisions.server.ts`) only proposes them, the action runs on the team's click in `answerEscalation` and `reopenEscalationDecision` undoes it, so the AI never changes operations on its own.
- Times agreed by the team (staff chat messages parsed by `parseAgreedTime`, or an approved early-release card) are persisted on the reservation as `guest_arrival_status` rows with `arrival_time_source='staff'` via `registerAgreedStayTime`, so every channel reads the same fact and the AI never reverts to the default time.
- Reservation change/cancel requests are routed by code (`reservation-change.ts`) per the booking's own cancellation modality: out-of-window or non-refundable → decline attributing the rule to the platform (with objection handling), in-window → team, unknown modality → platform menu; the AI never asks for new dates before a decision.
- Guest replies are never silenced by pending cards; the only silence is the 10-min pause after a direct staff message (`isPausedNow`), after which `humanLineGuidance` makes the AI reread and continue the staff's line without contradicting it.
- Delivering a team decision (`proactiveTrigger: "human_answer"`) strips escalation/handoff tools so the AI never re-asks; every automatic AI reply to a guest message on Airbnb first claims `claimAiReplySlot` (never inside `deliverToAirbnbThread`, so team answers, redo and proactive messages are always sent) (unique `ops_push_log` key per last guest message), so concurrent paths never send two replies to one question.
- Team answers to escalations are delivered via `deliverHumanAnswer` (`human-loop/deliver-answer.server.ts`): each attempt is claimed in `ops_push_log` and tracked in `context_snapshot.delivery`, and the 1-min stalled-replies cron retries (max 3) when the click timed out; the unanswered sweep skips conversations with a pending escalation so the team is never notified twice.
- Only a recent (<2h) operational pending card silences AI replies or new team questions (`cardSilencesAi` in `human-loop/silence-gate.ts`); knowledge/learning cards (`decision.kind='fact'`, `host_learning`) never do, so a stale card can never mute a guest.
